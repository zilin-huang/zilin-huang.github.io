🚐 We demonstrated our autonomous vehicle research to Wisconsin congressional staff.

November 14, 2025

2025

On November 14, 2025, we welcomed members of Wisconsin’s congressional delegation staff to UW–Madison during a campus visit organized by the university’s Office of Federal Relations.

During the engineering portion of the visit, our team at Sky-Lab, together with colleagues from the CATS Lab, introduced our autonomous research vehicle and its onboard sensing, computing, and control systems. We shared how connected and autonomous vehicle research can support safer, more efficient, and more accessible transportation.

More about the broader campus visit is available in UW–Madison Federal Relations’ report.
