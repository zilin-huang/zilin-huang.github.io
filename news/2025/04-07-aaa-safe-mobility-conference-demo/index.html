🚐 We hosted a live autonomous vehicle demonstration at the 2025 AAA Safe Mobility Conference.

April 7, 2025

2025

On April 7, 2025, our team at Sky-Lab, together with colleagues from the CATS Lab, hosted a live demonstration of our electric autonomous research van for more than 50 attendees at the 2025 AAA Safe Mobility Conference in Madison, Wisconsin. The audience included transportation professionals from industry, academia, and public agencies.

During the demonstration, we introduced the vehicle’s integrated hardware and software systems and showcased technologies developed by our team, including autonomous driving, path following, collision avoidance, and traffic-signal recognition. Attendees also viewed the onboard sensing and computing setup and experienced the system operating in a real-world environment.
