I gave an oral presentation on our paper, [A Novel WiFi-Oriented RSSI Signal Processing Method for Tracking Low-Speed Pedestrians](/publications/#huang2019wifi), at the 5th International Conference on Transportation Information and Safety (ICTIS 2019) in Liverpool, UK. Many thanks to my coauthors and to everyone who joined the discussion! 🎤
