This summer, I wrapped up my time as a Ph.D. student in the [Lyles School of Civil Engineering](https://engineering.purdue.edu/CCE) at [Purdue University](https://www.purdue.edu/), where I also worked with the [Center for Connected and Automated Transportation (CCAT)](https://ccat.umtri.umich.edu/). Next, I will continue my Ph.D. journey at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://www.wisc.edu/). 🚂

I am deeply grateful to Prof. [Samuel Labi](https://engineering.purdue.edu/CCE/People/Faculty/Labi) for the warm welcome to Purdue and for the generous guidance and support along the way. I also sincerely thank Prof. [Sikai (Sky) Chen](https://directory.engr.wisc.edu/cee/Faculty/Chen_Sikai/) for the mentorship and trust as I prepared for the next step. To my friends and colleagues at Purdue: **thank you** for making my time in West Lafayette so warm and memorable.

My time at Purdue was short, but the friendships and lessons from this summer will stay with me. Boiler Up, and here’s to the next chapter! 💗
