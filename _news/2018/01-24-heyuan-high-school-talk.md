On January 24, 2018, I was honored to be invited to Heyuan High School in Heyuan, Guangdong, to give a talk to the students. 🎤

I shared my experiences from university life and encouraged the students as they prepared for the next stage of their journey. Thank you to the teachers and students for the warm welcome! 💗
